FAQ

Common questions

How long does setup take?

Most schools are fully working within a week. Classes, fee structures and student data are set up with you, and staff learn the system in a single short session.

How do parents pay fees?

Parents can pay at the school office, by bank transfer, EasyPaisa or JazzCash. Every payment is recorded against the student's challan and a receipt is issued instantly.

Can it print result cards for our board?

Yes. Marks are entered once per exam and SmartSchool generates printable result cards with subject totals, percentage, grade and class position.

Can we manage multiple branches?

Yes — multi-branch management with a combined dashboard and branch-level reporting is part of the Premium and Custom plans.

Can we export our data?

Any time. Students, attendance, fees, results and accounts export to CSV/Excel, and a full backup of your data can be downloaded from inside the app.

Is there a contract?

No long-term lock-in. Premium is billed quarterly in advance and can be cancelled with one month's notice.
